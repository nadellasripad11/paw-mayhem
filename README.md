# paw mayhem

**small cats. big knockback. total chaos.**

paw mayhem is a roblox shooter where you play as a cat, grab a blaster, and try to knock everyone else off floating islands.

every hit you land raises the other cat's knockback %, so the more someone gets hit, the further the next shot sends them. you don't win by draining health, you win by launching people off the map. it gets chaotic fast, which is kind of the point.

the loop is simple: shoot, stack up knockback, launch them, watch them fall off.

---

## what's in it

**fighting**
- duels from 1v1 up to 5v5. you queue by standing on a pad in the lobby, and if nobody shows up a bot fills in after 15 seconds
- big arena matches on sky islands, volcano, toybox and rooftops, with team deathmatch, free for all, king of the hill, ringout and capture the flag
- four duel maps: meadow, sakura garden, autumn ridge and frost peaks
- 10 blasters with skins, plus dash, pounce, multi jump and fly
- the server checks every shot, hit and reward, so nothing can be cheated from the client

**the lobby**
- a matchmaking hall, a shooting range, a weapons room and a market, all walkable
- cat customisation, emotes, daily tasks, a season pass, levels, coins, badges and leaderboards

**no toolbox models.** every character, map, ui element and effect is built in code or generated by my own scripts.

## the parts i'm proud of

- the end of every match. the final score counts up, then victory drops in letter by letter with light rays, the winners' avatars and confetti cannons
- the duel vs intro and the knockout stamps
- the controls, which work the same on phone and laptop: clean glassy buttons with cooldowns that drain and glow when ready
- most of the art comes from my own code. the lobby columns, the arch, the chandeliers, the trees and rocks on the maps and all 10 guns are 3d models built by scripts in `tools/`, with their textures made the same way

## what was hard

making it look good without modelling everything by hand. that's why i ended up writing the model and texture generators, plus a one-paste studio setup that imports them. i also wrote tests that fake roblox, so i could check the setup without opening studio.

the aiming was a pain too. shots kept drifting off the crosshair, and it turned out three separate bugs were stacking up: the camera ray clipping things behind the cat, recoil moving the aim, and spread growing while you held fire.

## how it's organised

```
src/
  ReplicatedStorage/Shared/   config, remotes, utilities, character building
  ServerScriptService/Server/ services, the lobby, duels and the maps
  StarterPlayer/.../Client/   controllers and every ui screen
tools/                        model and texture generators, tests, code checks
```

weapons, cats, progression and match rules all live in `src/ReplicatedStorage/Shared/Config/`, so the whole feel of the game can be tuned from one folder.

the server owns everything that matters: shooting, hit detection, knockback and rewards. the client sends what the player did and draws the result.

## play it

- roblox.com/games/94864734806530, on pc or phone
- the quickest way in is to walk into the matchmaking hall and stand on a duel pad. a bot joins after 15 seconds
- pc: wasd to move, mouse to aim, click to shoot, space to jump (twice to double jump), q to dash, e to pounce, v to switch the camera
- phone: the on-screen buttons
- the shooting range behind the spawn is good for trying the guns on target cats
