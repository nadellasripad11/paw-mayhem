# paw mayhem

a roblox party shooter where you play as cats and try to knock each other off floating islands.

every hit you land raises the other cat's knockback %, so the more you hit someone the further they fly. you don't really win by draining health, you win by launching people off the map. it gets chaotic fast, which is kind of the point.

## what's in it

- duels from 1v1 up to 5v5. you queue by standing on a pad in the lobby, and if nobody shows up a bot fills in after 15 seconds
- big arena matches on sky islands, volcano, toybox and rooftops, with team deathmatch, free for all, king of the hill, ringout and capture the flag
- four duel maps: meadow, sakura garden, autumn ridge and frost peaks
- 10 blasters with skins, plus dash, pounce, multi jump and fly
- a proper lobby with a matchmaking hall, shooting range, weapons room and market
- cat customisation, emotes, daily tasks, a season pass, levels, coins, badges and leaderboards
- the server checks every shot, hit and reward, so nothing can be cheated from the client

## the parts i'm proud of

- the end of every match. the final score counts up, then victory drops in letter by letter with light rays, the winners' avatars and confetti cannons
- the duel vs intro and the knockout stamps
- the controls, which work like rivals on both phone and laptop: clean glassy buttons with cooldowns that drain and glow when ready
- most of the art is generated by my own code. the lobby columns, the arch, the chandeliers, the trees and rocks on the maps and all 10 guns are built as 3d models by scripts in `tools/`, with their textures made the same way

## what was hard

making it look good without modelling everything by hand. that's why i ended up writing the model and texture generators, plus a one-paste studio setup that imports them (with tests that fake roblox so i could check the setup without opening studio).

the aiming was a pain too. shots kept drifting off the crosshair, and it turned out three separate bugs were stacking: the camera ray clipping things behind the cat, recoil moving the aim, and spread growing while you held fire.

## how to test it

- play it at roblox.com/games/94864734806530 on pc or phone
- the quickest way in is to walk into the matchmaking hall and stand on a duel pad. a bot joins after 15 seconds
- pc: wasd to move, mouse to aim, click to shoot, space to jump (twice to double jump), q to dash, e to pounce, v to switch the camera
- phone: the on-screen buttons
- the shooting range behind the spawn is good for trying the guns on target cats

## running it from the code

you need [rojo](https://rojo.space). then:

```
rojo serve
```

open roblox studio, connect the rojo plugin and press play. the full walkthrough is in [GAME_SETUP.md](GAME_SETUP.md), and multiplayer testing is in [TESTING.md](TESTING.md).

the 3d models get imported into studio once. the steps are in [docs/LOBBY_ART.md](docs/LOBBY_ART.md) and [docs/GAME_MODELS.md](docs/GAME_MODELS.md).

## where things are

- `src/ReplicatedStorage/Shared` is shared config, remotes and character building. weapons, cats, progression and match rules all live in `Config/`, so they're easy to tweak
- `src/ServerScriptService/Server` is the server: services, the lobby, duels and the maps
- `src/StarterPlayer/StarterPlayerScripts/Client` is the client: controllers and every ui screen
- `tools/` has the model and texture generators, their tests and some code checks
